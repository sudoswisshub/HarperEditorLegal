No, never. Proofmark is built to be 100% private and offline. All grammar and spelling checks are processed directly on your Mac by a bundled local grammar engine. Your text is never sent anywhere, and the app requires no cloud account or internet connection.

Proofmark supports plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) files. You can open, write, edit, and save files natively with full support for macOS autosave, recent documents, and Find/Replace.

You can add words directly from any issue suggestion menu in your document, or navigate to Settings → Dictionary inside the app. You can manage terms individually or toggle "Edit as text" to paste multiple terms at once.

Open Settings → General and choose your preferred English dialect from the menu: American, British, Canadian, Australian, or Indian English. Proofmark instantly updates its rules and spelling recommendations.

Proofmark requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or later. The bundled grammar engine is compiled natively for both Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) and Intel Mac computers.
