Privacy Policy

Harper Editor is engineered from the ground up to keep your documents and writing completely confidential.

Effective Date: July 26, 2026

🔒 Core Guarantee: Harper Editor makes zero network calls. Your text never leaves your Mac, and no account or sign-up is required.

1. Data Collection & Analytics

Harper Editor does not collect, track, aggregate, or sell any of your personal information, document contents, keystrokes, usage data, telemetry, advertising identifiers, or crash reports.

We do not use third-party analytics frameworks, advertising SDKs, or remote logging services. There are no user accounts, login portals, or cloud syncing components.

2. Local On-Device Processing

All grammar, spelling, style, and readability checks are performed locally on your Mac using an embedded, offline binary of harper-ls. The application binary executes inside your local macOS environment.

When you open or write a document in Harper Editor, the contents are processed in-memory by the local bundled helper process via standard input/output (LSP). Text is never transmitted across the network, internet, or to external cloud servers.

3. Document Access & Storage

Harper Editor only accesses files that you explicitly open, edit, or save using standard macOS file dialogs or Finder actions.

  • Document Files: Plain text (.txt) and Markdown (.md) files remain in the directories you choose on your local drive or iCloud Drive.
  • App Preferences: Your selected English dialect, active grammar rules, line height, font preferences, and custom dictionary terms are stored in your Mac's secure app sandbox container (~/Library/Containers/...).

4. Retention & Deletion

You retain total ownership and control over your data:

  • You can delete your custom dictionary terms individually or clear them in plain text format under Settings → Dictionary.
  • Removing the Harper Editor application and its sandbox container removes all locally stored app preferences.
  • Your original files remain intact on your Mac until you decide to move or delete them via Finder.

5. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, we will update this Privacy Policy publicly prior to releasing any updated version of the software. Because the app operates offline without network collection, updates will be reflected in future release notes and on this website.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Support Page or email us at alice.turcanu91@gmail.com.
